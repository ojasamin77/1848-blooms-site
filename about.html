Flowers for the room and the people in it.

1848 Blooms & Designs is a floral design studio serving Delaware and Philadelphia. We create florals for weddings and events, and teach hands-on floral experiences.

Thoughtful design

We begin with questions about the space, the season and how you want people to feel. The flowers follow from the answers, never from a template.

Intentional details

A ribbon color, a stem height, the way a bouquet sits in the hand. We decide each one on purpose, and we check them against each other.

Unforgettable moments

Good florals do their work quietly. They become the thing guests mention afterward, without ever getting in the way of the day.

How an event comes together.

Every project follows the same four steps, whether it is one bouquet or a full wedding. [Confirm this process and add typical timelines.]

  1. Inquire

    Tell us the date, the place and what you are imagining. We reply with availability.

  2. Consult

    We talk through the space, your palette and your priorities, and look at inspiration together.

  3. Design

    You receive a proposal with florals, quantities and pricing. We refine it with you until it feels right.

  4. Deliver and set up

    We prepare the flowers, bring them to your venue and install them, so you can focus on everything else.
