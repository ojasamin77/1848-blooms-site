Pick your stems. Arrange them. Take them home.

The Floral Bar brings a self-serve flower station to your event. Guests choose their own blooms, build a bouquet or arrangement, and leave with something they made.

Inquire about a Floral Bar

How it works.

  1. Pick

    We set up the bar with seasonal stems, greenery and containers in your palette. Guests choose what they love.

  2. Arrange

    A designer is on hand to show guests how to build a bouquet or arrangement, whatever their experience.

  3. Take

    Everyone leaves with their own piece, wrapped and ready to carry home. It doubles as a favor.

Where it works best.

  • Bridal showers and bachelorette parties
  • Weddings and rehearsal dinnersPart of the reception, or a favor guests make.
  • Birthdays and baby showers
  • Corporate and team events
